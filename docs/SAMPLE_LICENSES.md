## External sample sources

User-added sample URLs, credits and license statements are supplied by users;
the app does not verify ownership or grant permission. Importing a source file
only stores metadata. RUN can download its map and the audio from its owner.
Sharing code includes the loader and credits, not a copy of that audio.
Check the source's terms for downloads, use in recordings and redistribution.

RolandTR808/tidal-drum-machines is not bundled or loaded by default: no clear
redistribution license was found in its repository during this review.
A public download URL is not by itself redistribution permission.

Steam distribution still requires the AGPL corresponding source and notices.
Recheck compatibility before adding Steam DRM or changing distribution terms.
This records identified permissions, not a guarantee of legal clearance.
